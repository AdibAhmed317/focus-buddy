Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Focus Buddy ("Extension," "we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Chrome extension.

2. Information We Collect

Focus Buddy collects ZERO personal information.

We do not:

  • Collect browsing history
  • Track which websites you visit
  • Store personal data
  • Use analytics or tracking cookies
  • Share data with third parties
  • Require you to create an account

3. Local Storage

Focus Buddy stores your preferences locally on your device using Chrome's storage API. This includes:

  • Your timer interval settings (min/max minutes)
  • Your selected sound preference
  • Any custom audio files you upload
Important: This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to any server, not backed up to the cloud, and not shared with anyone. Only the Focus Buddy extension can access it.

4. Permissions Explained

"storage": Allows the extension to save your preferences (timer intervals, selected sounds) locally on your device using Chrome's storage API.

"tabs": Allows the extension to open the focus timer in a new browser tab when you click "Open in Tab."

"windows": Allows the extension to create a dedicated focus window for the timer when you click "Open Focus Window."

5. Audio Files

If you upload custom audio files, they are:

  • Stored locally on your device only
  • Never uploaded to any server
  • Never shared with third parties
  • Deleted when you uninstall the extension

6. Third-Party Services

Focus Buddy does not use any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

The extension is self-contained and communicates only with your browser.

7. Data Security

Your data is secure because:

  • It never leaves your device
  • It's protected by your device's security
  • Only the Focus Buddy extension can access it
  • No network transmission occurs

8. Deleting Your Data

To delete all data associated with Focus Buddy:

  • Uninstall the extension from Chrome
  • All stored preferences and custom audio files will be permanently deleted
  • You can also clear extension data in Chrome Settings → Privacy and Security → Clear browsing data → Cookies and other site data

9. Children's Privacy

Focus Buddy does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, please contact us immediately.
